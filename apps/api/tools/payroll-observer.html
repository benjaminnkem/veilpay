VeilPay Payroll Observer

A beginner-friendly, read-only dashboard for checking each Safe funding and confidential payroll step. It never stores tokens, salaries, seed phrases, or decrypted balances.

What this page can prove: Safe deployment, funding-handle existence, employee readiness, payroll state, Safe approval, execution receipt, and an employee’s own wallet-authorized balance.
What it cannot do: decrypt the company Safe’s confidential ctUSDC balance. The Safe contract—not its owner EOA—holds that Nox ACL permission.
